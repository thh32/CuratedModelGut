## What is CuratedModelGut:
This is a repository of manually curated omics data from studies using in vitro model systems.

For each sample in each study, we collect metadata on the following FAIR data:
- 16S rRNA amplicon
- ITS amplicon
- Shotgun metagenomes (WGS)
- Metatranscriptomes
- Metaproteomic
- Metabolomic (targetted and untargetted)

As a curated collection of FAIR data, this resource focuses on including only data which can be reused by the research community. As such, if data is unavailable, such as metabolomic data used in a paper but not released, then it is marked as not present in this resource. 


## The Focus of CuratedModelGut
### Background
While the gut microbiome is known to influence human health and disease, direct methods to study this community remain difficult. In vitro systems provide a method to directly modify the environment of the microbiota (food, pH, flow speed, oxygen, etc.). A range of in vitro model gut systems have been developed, each with a myriad of different factors which influence the results they give, including basic features such as the media used to feed the microbiota. These factors often remain confined to the methods, instead of seen as critical aspects of an experiment which influences the microbiota-effects being studied.

### The Goal
This is a resource for the community. We aim to provide high-quality metadata for the samples we curate by applying the four eyes principle. By providing this resource, we hope the research community can address questions such as "which factors influence the microbiota the most?" "which species are unable to colonise in vitro systems and why?".

## INFOGUT (CA23110)
The CuratedModelGut was initiated as part of INFOGUT (https://infogut.eu/), an EU COST action (CA23110) focused on the standardisation of in vitro model gut experiments. Within INFOGUT, Working group 4 is focused on the bioinformatic analysis and storage of data, including making data FAIR for long term use and reuse. If you wish to join INFOGUT, please register on the COST website (https://www.cost.eu/actions/CA23110/).
